## Primary sources: new models

### Official model pages (most reliable for open-weight releases)
Some labs publish weights here before, or without, a blog announcement. Z.ai and DeepSeek post their technical write-ups here too.
- DeepSeek: https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai
- Qwen (Alibaba): https://huggingface.co/Qwen
- Moonshot AI (Kimi): https://huggingface.co/moonshotai
- Z.ai (Zhipu, GLM): https://huggingface.co/zai-org

- Mistral AI: https://huggingface.co/mistralai
- Google (Gemma): https://huggingface.co/google

## Community (discovery only: follow through to the primary source)
- r/LocalLLaMA: fastest, most rigorous discussion of new open-weight models
- r/MachineLearning: research discussion, strictly moderated
- Hacker News: informed first impressions of new models and tools: https://news.ycombinator.com
- r/AI_Agents: honest build logs and post-mortems, but high noise and self-promotion; lowest priority
Reddit pages often can't be fetched. AINews (on Latent Space) covers the main AI subreddits daily and is the better route.

## Avoid
- Documentation, changelogs, release-note pages, help-center articles, spec pages and API references. They are reference material to look up when needed, not reading.
- Lookalike or mirror domains of official sites. Use the official domain only.
- Crypto and trading news sites reposting AI announcements.
- Near-duplicate, auto-generated tutorials on dev.to, Medium, and similar platforms that restate launch details.
- Speculative "release tracker" or rumor pages about unreleased models.
- "Best AI sources" lists published by companies promoting their own product.
- r/singularity and other hype-driven communities.
- Papers with Code (shut down in 2025; now redirects to Hugging Face).
- MCP server directories and marketplaces (mcp.so, Smithery, Glama, and similar) as sources of servers to install. Articles about them are fine to read.
