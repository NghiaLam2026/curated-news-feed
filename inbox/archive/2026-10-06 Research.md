### 3. [Attention Tax, Handoff Tax: A Stylised Model of When Multi-Agent LLM Systems Help](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06069)
- **Source:** arXiv (preprint) · published 2026-10-05
- **Matches:** multi-agent coordination (core)
- **Why it's here:** Models the trade-off between context degradation that decomposition avoids and the "handoff tax" of compressing state between agents, to explain when multi-agent beats single-agent. Calibrated on a ledger-reconciliation task, it predicted a crossover around depth 10, and decomposition then won at deeper tasks close to the predicted rates.

### 5. [I stopped reviewing my agents' code. Here's what I do instead.](https://alexeyindeev.substack.com/p/i-stopped-reviewing-my-agents-code)
- **Source:** The Leveraged Mind (Alexey Indeev) · published 2026-10-04
- **Matches:** orchestrator-worker, long-running agents, human-in-the-loop (core)
- **Why it's here:** A practitioner case study of 562 merged PRs in two weeks: the human reviews the plan rather than the diff, a coordinator agent holds only plan and status while sub-agents do the work, and small PRs flow through tests, review bots and a merge queue. Oversight comes via HTML progress reports, and token capacity turned out to be the real bottleneck.

### 7. [VeriHarness: Scaling Agentic Verification for Long-Horizon Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00972)
- **Source:** arXiv (preprint; Cambridge and Google Cloud AI Research) · published 2026-10-01
